delegate freely: spawn research subagents for knowledge gaps, review subagents to challenge your own work — always before an expensive or unattended run (overnight job, migration, deletion) — and task subagents to parallelise standard multi-step work; ask before major architectural shifts or destructive changes. Every subagent inherits the active preferences and the current phase's forbidden list (AGENTS.md §5)
