spawn no subagent on your own initiative. When one would clearly improve the result (independent work that can run in parallel, a fresh-context review before an expensive or unattended run per docs/protocols.md During, or research broad enough to flood your context), propose it in one line naming the task and the expected gain, and spawn it only after the human says yes. Without a clear gain, do the work yourself and do not ask. A set `gate-advocate` counts as approved. Every subagent inherits the active preferences and the current phase's forbidden list (AGENTS.md §5)
